Circular icon-only button: the arrow on service cards, the hero play button, small utilities.

- Props: `icon` (an `Icon` name), `label` (required — becomes `aria-label`), `variant` `outline` | `solid` | `light` (white with float shadow, over imagery) | `paper`, `size` `sm` 36 | `md` 44 | `lg` 56, optional `href`.
- Always a circle. Use `light` + `lg` + `play` for video.
