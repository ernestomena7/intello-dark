White card for one service: illustration well, title, two bullets and a round arrow — the whole card is the link.

- Props: `title` (2–4 words), `bullets` (exactly two, ≤ 4 words each), `icon` or `image`, `href`.
- Lay four in `div.itl-services` (4 columns, 2 below 900px).

**Overlay variant (`variant="overlay"`, needs `image`).** The photograph fills the card (400px minimum height) and the title, bullets and arrow sit on a dark glass panel at the bottom; bullet points turn yellow. Use it on dark-style pages; keep the default card on light pages.
